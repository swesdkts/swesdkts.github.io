description: >
 Descendants of DeWitty is a narrative-driven historical mystery game centered on the real history of DeWitty, once the largest African-American settlement in the Midwest. As Lead Developer during a 1.5-year internship, I built the playable experience end-to-end in Unity, creating environments, animations, shaders, and core gameplay systems, including a custom notebook-based dialogue system and real-time environmental effects such as a flowing river shader. Although the project did not ship due to a shift in organizational priorities, the showcased build represents my solo technical and creative ownership of the game.
